# Changelog

## 1.4.0

- Group timers into Consumables, Party & Raid, and Personal, with a category dropdown in `/ct tracking`.
- Add an option to hide the default Well Fed and Weapon Oil trackers.
- Add a per-character, per-spec **On-Click Settings** tab for food and flasks. Show food counts on the icon and allow direct clicks on supported items and helpful spells outside combat.

## 1.3.0

- Show currently active buffs as faded rows in `/ct tracking` and add them with a green checkmark.

## 1.2.3

- Show **In Combat** on timers whose data is restricted during combat, with customizable wording under **Display text**.

## 1.2.2

- Show the spell name between its icon and ID in `/ct tracking`.
- Show an on-screen and chat warning when `/ct` or `/ct tracking` is used in combat.

## 1.2.1

- Show buff icons beside spell IDs in the `/ct tracking` window.

## 1.2.0

- Add `/ct tracking` to list, add, and remove extra tracked spell IDs in one window.

## 1.1.0

- Show missing timer text in red by default, with preset color swatches, a color picker, and a hex field.
- Add a padlock to the timer window to lock or unlock the timer and warning positions outside combat.

## 1.0.0

- Add countdowns for Well Fed and temporary weapon effects, plus custom buff tracking by spell ID.
- Add optional missing-buff warnings and controls for combat, world, dungeon, and raid visibility.
- Add customizable display text and draggable timer and warning positions.
